## A person approves, a person sends

Two things the portal could not do until now: ask a supplier for a renewal by
email, and send a customer their certificates from an order a person built.
Both wait for a person before anything leaves.

### Added

- [config] **The portal can ask a supplier to renew a document.** When a document enters its renewal window, a request to the supplier is drafted from a fixed template and waits for one approval. Nothing reaches a supplier until a person approves it, and the exact text sent is kept. Reminders are drafted on the day of expiry and 7 and 14 days after; at 21 days the portal stops and tells your admins. It does nothing until a supplier has a document contact.
- [config] **Supplier contacts.** Each supplier has a Contacts tab for the person who keeps their documents current. A supplier list import fills it from the contact email column.
- [config] **A default owner for renewals** (Settings > Owner Routing). Approvals fall to this person when a record's owner has no login.
- [existing] **Supplier emails come from "Your organisation via SupDox"**, and replies go to the person who approved the send.
- [existing] **Orders you fill by hand.** Create an order, pick the customer and ship date, and add COAs from Documents ("Add to order") or by searching inside the order. Each line shows product, lot and production date before anything is sent.
- [existing] **COAs go to the customer as attachments.** The review screen shows exactly what will be attached. A large set is split into numbered emails, and the split is shown first. A certificate that covers several lots is sent whole when the original is on file. Every send is listed under Sent documents.
- [existing] **Approved items** (Admin). Every item-and-supplier pair now has an approval status, separate from "currently supplied". Everything on file starts as approved, marked as a starting value and not as anyone's decision. The supplier list import's Approved column sets it and never overrides a person.
- [config] **Supplier facilities.** Add a supplier's plants under its Facilities tab and attach an item to one.
- [existing] **Brand owner, producer and plant code** can be edited on the product page. An item whose brand owner and producer differ is marked private label.
- [config] **Customer contacts and COA requirements by item.** Tick which contacts receive COAs, and record per item whether a COA is required and what it must show. The order review screen pre-fills those contacts and warns when a required item has no certificate. Nothing is blocked.

### Changed

- [existing] **Changing a renewal date after approval now asks why**, and records who changed it. Clearing the date means "this document does not renew", and the screen says so.
- [existing] **Search respects module switches.** With Order Fulfillment off, search no longer returns orders or customers.
- [existing] **A document emailed in no longer triggers an automatic reply to an outside sender.** The sender is answered only if they are a user of your organisation; otherwise your admins get a notice.
- [existing] **The auto-ingest and auto-approve switches are gone from the screens.** They never did anything: nothing is filed without a person approving it.
- [new-orgs] **A new organisation starts with a general extraction context** instead of the dairy one. The dairy text is a template you can load in Settings. Existing organisations keep exactly what they had.
- [existing] Example lot, PO and order numbers shown on empty screens and in help are now made up.

### Fixed

- [existing] **Merging two suppliers no longer deletes the merged supplier's requests, upload links, requirement checklist, spec limits or watch settings.** They move to the supplier that is kept.
- [existing] **A connector re-sending an order no longer fails on, or overwrites, lines a person has decided.**
- [existing] The split original of a multi-lot COA is now kept permanently, not for 90 days.

## Ask it any way, and it says what it found

Search is where everything the portal reads finally pays off, and it had two
halves that did not meet: one screen answered honestly ("nothing on file covers
that") but could not filter, the other could filter but only one filter at a
time, and silently ignored its own date filter. They now share one engine.
Filters stack in any order, a PO or invoice number or part of a lot number finds
the paper that states it, and a question with no covering document says so
instead of offering the nearest thing.

This release also carries the 27 September rulings on spec limits, product-level
requirements, and a round of fixes from the 20 September review.

### Added

- [existing] **Filters stack.** On Documents, supplier, then product, then type, then date narrow the list in any order; clearing one keeps the rest, and the counts beside each filter show what is left. Before this, only the first filter applied and the date filter was ignored.
- [existing] **Search by PO, invoice or part of a lot.** Our purchase order to a supplier (the K-number on their certificate) and a customer's PO on an order are both searchable, and kept apart because they are different numbers. "lot 104" reads as every lot starting 104 and says what that means in the supplier's own lot format.
- [existing] **Documents answers the question, not just the query.** When you search for a lot, a date or a PO, the Documents screen now leads with whether a document on file covers it, and says "nothing on file covers that" when none does.
- [existing] **A certificate that was already expired when it arrived says so** in the review queue, counts as a gap rather than satisfying its requirement, and alerts the QA route on approval.
- [existing] **A method or sample-size mismatch now alerts someone.** An MPN result against a CFU limit, or "absent in 10 g" against a 25 g requirement, is still never judged, but it is now emailed to the result's owner with the reason instead of waiting silently in the queue.
- [config] **Analytes can be given a category** (indicator organism, compositional, zero tolerance, regulatory ceiling), which gives a miss a band that fits it — a coliform count ten times over reads as a violation, a fat result slightly over reads as a light note. Bands order and word the alerts; they never change a verdict.
- [config] **A requirement can be owed per product.** Mark a requirement as per product and a supplier shipping twelve items owes twelve; a supplier cannot read as covered while one of its products has nothing set up. Individual products can be exempted with a reason, marked no longer supplied, or declared to owe nothing.
- [config] **Document Types can say which requirements a type closes**, outside the setup wizard, and Requirements shows which types close each one.
- [new-orgs] The starter pack marks ingredient and allergen statements, nutritionals, labels and spec sheets as owed per product, and the COA per lot.

### Changed

- [existing] Spec limit tiers now read **Critical / Major / Minor**, each with a plain-language explanation on hover. Stored values are unchanged, so every past result keeps its meaning. Holds are not enforced yet; the Critical explanation says so.
- [existing] Files sent to a customer are named from the document (supplier, type, lot or date) instead of carrying the name they were uploaded under, and the recipient page shows the sender's name and email.
- [new-orgs] Starter-pack requirement groups use neutral names (Product Specification, Food Safety Plans & Programs, and so on) instead of one customer's SOP number.

### Fixed

- [existing] Adding a product from a supplier's Products tab now links it to that supplier. It used to create the product unlinked, so it never appeared there and a second attempt failed with "slug already exists".

## Find a certificate by lot or production date, down to the exact row

- [existing] **A lot number is found however it is typed.** Search for 1042620303,
  10426203-03, or put the lot and the sublot in the new *Lot / sublot* boxes
  separately, and the same certificate comes back. The lot and the sublot are
  matched part against part, as the certificate prints them.
- [existing] **Each result names the lot row that matched.** A certificate that lists
  several lots now shows exactly which row answered your search, for example
  *Lot 10426203 · sublot 03 · produced Jul 22, 2026 · 50 EA · 2755.75 LB*.
- [existing] **Production date is a real field on every lot.** The date a lot was produced
  is now stored on the lot itself, with where it came from, and shown on the Lots
  page. Searching a production date returns exactly the rows produced that day.
- [existing] **Older certificates are shown as "likely — confirm".** Some older certificates
  had their production date saved as a code date. Those appear under *Likely
  covering — confirm*, with a note saying so, and are never counted as a
  confirmed match until someone opens them and checks.
- [existing] **A multi-lot certificate only matches on its own row.** A search for one lot
  and a date no longer brings back a certificate just because a different row on
  the same page had that date.

## Review Queue catches two date mistakes

- [existing] **A row produced after its expiry is flagged**, including when the expiry is
  printed once in the certificate's header rather than on the row.
- [existing] **One lot with two production dates is flagged** when a certificate lists the
  same lot and sublot twice with different production dates.

## Order the documents, not only the certificates

An order can now carry documents for the items a customer buys, pulled from
what is already on file, and each one leaves by its sharing rule.

### Added

- [existing] **Document orders.** On an order, choose items from the approved item list and the kinds of document wanted. The portal finds the current approved document for each item and supplier. An item with several approved suppliers gets a line for each; nothing is picked for you.
- [existing] **What happens at send is shown before you send.** Three groups: goes now, waits for QA, will not go. Send-freely documents go on one link that lasts 30 days. Certificates of analysis go attached, as before. Locked documents never go.
- [existing] **Waiting for QA.** A document that needs QA approval waits until a QA person releases or refuses it. Release shows the document, the item, who asked and the exact recipients first, and is refused if any of that changed since. The person who asked is told the outcome.
- [existing] **Missing and expired documents tell QA.** When an order asks for a document that is not on file or has expired, QA is told once. Nothing is sent or drafted to a supplier.
- [existing] **Private-label advisory.** A document for an item made by another producer under your brand carries a note to the person building the order that it names the producer. Nothing is blocked.
- [existing] **Read-only accounts can build an order** and add document lines. Sending still needs a full account.
- [config] **QA hears about it through the "QA" owner route.** With no route set, releases fall to the organisation's master user and then its administrators, and notices about missing or expired documents are recorded as unrouted.

### Changed

- [existing] **A resend checks every file again.** A document that was refused, removed from the order, replaced or has expired since the first attempt is left out, and the resend says which.
- [existing] **A send in which nothing left no longer reads "sent".** It reads "Withdrawn, nothing sent" or "Partly sent, the rest withdrawn".
- [existing] An order is marked delivered only when none of its document lines is still waiting.

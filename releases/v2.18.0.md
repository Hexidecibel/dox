## One place for product identity

- [existing] **One place for what a product goes by.** The supplier's names and item
  numbers for a product now live alongside our SKUs and aliases, and can be
  edited from either the product page or the supplier's new **Product
  identifiers** tab.
- [existing] **Certificate products not yet identified are listed** on that tab, with
  one-click mapping to one of our products.
- [existing] **Lot-match suggestions read the supplier's item number first**, then the
  product name and pack. When a name could mean more than one of our products,
  no product is picked, and the suggestion says why.
- [existing] **A confirmed item number that shows a certificate is for a different
  product no longer produces a suggestion.**
- [existing] **Mapping a product during review now records it as a confirmed
  identifier**, so it shows up on the product and supplier screens.

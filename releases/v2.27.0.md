## Every field, and your own examples

Search gets its Advanced mode: every filter as an editable row, counts that
update as filters stack, and results as documents, lots, products or suppliers.
Everything the portal records about a document can now be filtered on. The
examples under the search box come from your own documents, and search is
checked against a fixed set of about 140 real-shaped questions before every
release. This release also carries the next round of the 27 September rules.

### Added

- [existing] **Advanced search.** Every filter is an editable row you can include or exclude. The filter rail shows live counts as filters stack, and results can be viewed as documents, lots, products or suppliers, with a column chooser.
- [existing] **New filters:** requirement, claim, spec result, renewal state, classification, owner, how a document came in, approved date, document #, certificate #, customer. "Satisfies a requirement" and "triggers a claim" count only links a person has confirmed.
- [existing] **Our SKU, a pack size or a product's recorded name finds that product on its own.** Words that only describe a product ("butter") still need a date or lot beside them.
- [existing] **Search examples come from your own documents**: a real lot, a PO, a product and production day, an invoice, plus one labelled near miss. Each is run before it is shown, so every example finds something.
- [existing] **A split packet part says where it came from**: the packet's file name, the day it arrived and its page range, with a link to the original packet.
- [existing] **A spec sheet with no controlled-document markings** (revision, effective date, document number) is flagged in review as a possible sales sheet, with a one-click reject.
- [existing] **"Conflicts with your configuration" is shown apart from "verify by hand"**: a result reported in a kind of unit your limit is never measured in (MPN against CFU, percent against a count) leads the review banner.
- [config] **A document type can renew inside a fixed calendar window**, such as FDA food facility registration (October to December of even years).
- [config] **Saved searches keep their mode and columns, and an admin can share a view** with the whole organization.
- [new-orgs] The starter pack adds an FDA Food Facility Registration type with its window, and business license and FDA registration to every supplier's baseline.

### Changed

- [existing] "Approved" and "Came in by" are recorded for every document from now on; older documents show "Not recorded" unless an approval record proves them.
- [existing] Free-text search finds documents by the requirements they satisfy and by a product's confirmed identifiers, after a one-time background re-index.

### Fixed

- [existing] Renaming a supplier, product or type no longer drops four fields from the search index, and the re-index now runs on its own after an edit.
- [existing] A packet part's arrival date is the day the packet arrived, not the day it was split, so "expired on arrival" is judged fairly.

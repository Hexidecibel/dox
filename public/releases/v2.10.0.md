## Out of Spec says who judged a result, and where it was printed

- [existing] **Reviewed at approval, or found by the re-check over history.** Every result
  on the Out of Spec page now says how it got there: a reviewer saw it when the
  certificate was approved, or it came from the bulk re-check that ran your
  configured limits over certificates approved before the register existed.
  Results from the re-check carry a *Bulk re-check* chip, and a new filter
  shows one kind or the other.
- [existing] **Each result shows where on the certificate it came from.** A result now
  names its place on the page — the table, the row, and the lot it belongs
  to. A certificate covering several lots prints the same test once per lot,
  and those results used to look like duplicates of each other. They are now
  visibly separate results, one per lot.
- [existing] **"Checklist" is now "Requirements".** What a supplier owes you is called a
  requirement everywhere in the app — settings, supplier pages, requests and
  the setup wizard.

## Search understands products the way people name them

- **Name a product however you know it.** Search now recognises our SKU, the
  supplier's item number, a pack such as *300 gal tote* or *5 gallon bags*, and
  plain words such as *unsalted*, and says which product it resolved your search
  to.
- **When a phrase could mean more than one product, you see each one.** Search
  lists every product the phrase fits instead of quietly picking one.
- **A match that relies on an unconfirmed identifier says so.** Results that fit
  only through an identifier nobody has confirmed yet appear as *likely —
  confirm*, never as a confirmed match.
- **Pack weights match across units.** 55.115 lb and 25 kg are recognised as the
  same pack, and the conversion is shown next to the result.

## Search a warehouse order number to reach its certificates

- **An order number follows its lines to the certificates.** Only matches a
  person has accepted, or an exact lot row, count as covering the order.
- **A suggested match for a different product is flagged**, so a certificate for
  the wrong item is never taken as cover.

## Product identifiers

- **Product pages list every identifier the product goes by** — our SKU, supplier
  item numbers and names, packs and aliases — with where each came from. You can
  add, confirm, or remove them from the page.

## Extraction

- **The customer item number is read into its own field.** When a supplier prints
  our item number on a certificate, extraction now captures it as the customer
  item number rather than mixing it into another field.

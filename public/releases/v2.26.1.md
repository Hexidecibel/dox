## Ask for a month

Searching "butter produced in april" found nothing: search read a specific day
("April 14") but not a month or a range, so the whole phrase became a text search
for words no certificate prints. Months and ranges are now dates.

### Fixed

- [existing] **Search reads months and ranges.** "butter produced in april", "best by early May", "produced Apr 1–15", "since March" and "last month" become a date chip. A month without a year means that month in any year. Product words next to the date become a product chip, or "could mean N products" with nothing picked.

## Suppliers on watch, and nothing unjudged looks checked

- **A supplier can be put on watch.** Require extra analytes on that supplier's
  certificates of analysis, and set tighter limits for that supplier with a
  **review-by date**. When the date passes, the limit still applies and is
  flagged for review. It is never silently loosened.
- **Every result now shows one of five states:** *in spec*, *out of spec*,
  *could not check* (verify it by hand), *incomplete* (a required analyte was
  not reported), or *no limit configured*. A result nobody judged no longer
  looks like it was checked.
- **A new Test results panel on each document** shows every result and its
  state.
- **Out of Spec has Incomplete and No limit configured tabs.**
- **Settings › Spec Limits has a Suppliers on watch section**, and each
  supplier has a **Spec watch** tab.
- **A unit conversion used in a comparison is shown on the value**, and a
  comparison that was refused says why.

### Fixed
- Results reported per 0.1 g were read as per gram.

## What an outside page is allowed to show

Records has three pages a person outside your organisation opens with no
login: a public form, an update request, and a sign-off page. Each now sends
only what that page needs to be filled in or decided. This was checked by
automated tests and four rounds of independent review; nobody has yet clicked
through these screens in a browser.

### If you already use Records, this changes for you

- [existing] **Pick-lists on public forms are off.** A customer, supplier or product field on a public form used to arrive with your whole active list of that kind, for anyone holding the link. It is now a text box. What the person types is stored as text and shown in the grid as "not matched yet"; a person matches it, the portal does not guess.
- [existing] **A sign-off page shows only the record's title.** It used to show the first thirty fields of the record. Every existing step now shows the title and nothing else until the step names the fields to show.
- [existing] **The sender's email no longer appears on outside pages.** The update request and sign-off pages show the sender's name, or your organisation's name when they have none. The email that carries the link is unchanged.
- [existing] **Update requests no longer offer contact, document or linked-record fields.** A request or workflow step saved earlier that asked for one simply leaves it out. A customer, supplier or product is shown by name in a text box; an update request never has a pick-list.
- [existing] **Markup files are refused at public upload.** A file is judged by what it actually contains, not by what the browser says it is. SVG, HTML and similar files are refused. Word and Excel files are still accepted where the form allows them. Only images and PDFs open inline as a preview; anything else downloads.

### Added

- [config] **Publish a pick-list on a public form, one field at a time.** In the form builder, turn on "Let people pick from your list of ..." for a customer, supplier or product field. The builder warns that anyone with the link can read the names. Only names are published, never a customer number or product description.
- [config] **Choose which fields a sign-off page shows.** In the workflow step editor, pick the fields the approver should see beside the title. Calculated fields cannot be picked.
- [existing] **A workflow that has stopped moving says so.** A run that is in progress with no step waiting is shown as stalled, with the reason, and a signed-in person chooses Resume or Cancel. Nothing is restarted without someone asking.

### Changed

- [existing] **A record never leaves under an internal reference.** A title or a field shown on an outside page, or in the email that carries its link, is the name of your own record. An internal id is never shown, and a contact that is an email address is left out.
- [existing] **Every link is checked against one organisation.** A form, request or sign-off link only ever reads and writes a record of the organisation it belongs to. A link for an inactive organisation, or one with Records switched off, answers "not found", the same as a link that never existed.
- [existing] **A sign-off or update request closes with its workflow.** Cancelling a run, or pausing or archiving its workflow, closes the open links; reactivating the workflow reopens them. The row panel shows such a request as "Paused with its workflow".
- [existing] **What an outsider sends is checked by field type.** A number must be a number, a dropdown answer must be one of the options, and a record can be picked only from a list the form published.
- [existing] **Each opening of an outside page is recorded in the audit log and rate limited.** Five refused answers to an update request in an hour make that address wait.

### Fixed

- [existing] **Two decisions sent at the same moment count once.** The second gets "not found" and the workflow moves one step, not two.
- [existing] **Starting a workflow that cannot start now says why**, instead of reporting a run that had already been cancelled. A step that cannot be sent stops its run visibly and tells the owner.
- [existing] **A Records attachment with a name in any script downloads.** Names in Chinese or Cyrillic, or with an emoji, used to fail.
- [existing] **Documents sent from search, order documents, renewal requests and alerts read names and recipients only from your own organisation.** Nothing in the portal writes a record that points at another organisation's supplier, product, document type or user, so this was not a live leak; the pages and emails now refuse to print such a name, or mail such a person, if one ever existed.

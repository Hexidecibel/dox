## Spec checks read more of how certificates write units

- [existing] **ppm, mg/kg, µg/g, ppb and mg/100g now convert automatically** when a
  result and a limit are written in different ones of these. A converted
  comparison always says it was converted, so you can see the arithmetic.
- [existing] **mg/L, log counts and cell counts are compared only with their own kind.**
  A result in one of these is never judged against a limit written in another
  unit; it shows as could not be checked instead.
- [existing] **"oz" is treated as ambiguous** unless the certificate says fl oz or oz wt.
- [existing] **A count written "per mL" with no counting method is recognised but not
  assumed to be CFU.** It is reported as could not be checked rather than
  judged against a CFU limit.
- [existing] **A combined yeast & mold result is never checked against a separate mold or
  yeast limit, and separate yeast and mold results are never added together.**
  More spellings of yeast & mold are recognised.

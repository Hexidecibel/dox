## Your name, your colours, on what leaves

An organisation can now put its own name, logo, colours and support line on
the pages outsiders open and on the mail that leaves the portal.

### Added

- [config] **Settings, then Brand.** Set a display name, a logo, a primary and an accent colour, and a support line (text, email, phone). A live preview shows the page header and the mail header before you save.
- [config] **Where it shows.** On the pages people outside your organisation open: the documents page a recipient gets, the supplier request page, alert pages, Records forms, update requests and sign-off pages, and the file drop. And on mail that leaves: documents sent from search, order documents, supplier renewal requests, and Records update and sign-off requests. The display name is also the name the mail is from. The address it is sent from, and where replies go, are unchanged.
- [config] **A different support line per page.** The line shown on, say, the supplier request page can differ from the default.
- [config] **Logos.** PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 512 KB, not animated. A logo that was published can be withdrawn with a reason; mail already sent will then show a broken image. Up to 10 are kept.
- [existing] **Nothing changes until a brand is set.** An organisation with no brand looks exactly as before, on every page and in every mail. No brand has been set for anyone. Removing a brand returns to that look.
- [existing] Mail to your own people (invitations, password resets, alerts, QA notices) is not branded.

### Changed

- [existing] **A Records form's accent colour must be a real colour** (six-digit hex; a three-digit one is accepted and written out in full). The form builder now has a colour picker. No existing form changes colour.
- [existing] The name mail is sent from no longer carries invisible or text-reversing characters.

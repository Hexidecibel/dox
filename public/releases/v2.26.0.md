## Type it the way you would say it

Search and Documents are now one search. Type a lot, a date, a PO or an invoice
the way you would say it, and the portal shows what it read as chips under the
box before it answers. The answer comes first: covered, likely and worth
confirming, or nothing on file covers it. The certificate opens beside the
results with the answering lot row named.

### Added

- [existing] **Search reads your words as chips.** Lots, dated phrases (production, code or best-by), POs, invoices and orders appear under the box as you type. Click a chip to change how it was read, or turn it back into plain words, which are never re-read.
- [existing] **The answer comes first.** A card leads with Covered, Likely · confirm, Nothing covers, or Could mean several products. Below it: covering results, then likely ones, then nearby ones folded away, then files still in the Review Queue. What can be exported is unchanged.
- [existing] **✦ Ask AI is a button (or Ctrl/⌘+Enter).** Its reading comes back as chips marked ✦, each with the reason it was read that way.
- [existing] **The certificate opens beside the results** with the answering lot row named, and each result dims the other lots on the same certificate.
- [existing] **Ctrl/⌘-K opens search from any page**, showing how your words will be read and which suppliers, products and documents they name.

### Changed

- [existing] **Documents and Search are one search.** Documents gets the same answers, chips, preview and export bar, and keeps its filters.
- [existing] Search, notifications and help now sit in a header across the top of every page instead of floating over it.

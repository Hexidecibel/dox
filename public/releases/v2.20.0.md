## Take the documents with you

- **Select results in search and take them with you.** Tick the documents you
  want and a bar appears showing how many are selected. Only covering results
  are selected by default — a nearby result has to be included on purpose — and
  the selection survives your next search, so you can gather documents from
  several queries before sending them.
- **Download a ZIP with a manifest of what's inside.** The archive holds the
  current version of every selected document plus a manifest listing, per file,
  the document, supplier, document type, lot and production date.
- **Or send them to someone as a link that expires in 30 days.** The mail goes
  out from the portal with your address as the reply-to, so a reply comes back
  to you, and you can say who you are sending on behalf of. The recipient gets
  a page listing the documents, and can take the whole set or one file at a
  time. No login, no attachments.
- **Every export is recorded**, including who received a link and when they
  opened it.

## Also in this release

- **Documents are now marked classified or needing review as they are
  approved**, so the ones nobody has really ruled on are visible as such.
- **GraphQL says when something is forbidden or not found** instead of
  answering "Unexpected error".
- **The help and the API reference no longer describe features that were never
  built.** The Reports export is audited, and it exports the whole report
  rather than the page on screen.

## Renewal warnings on your schedule, and requirements from your supplier list

### When owners are warned about renewals

- [config] **Choose how far ahead owners are warned** that a document is coming due,
  for the whole organization on Settings › Owner Routing, and per document
  type on Document Types (for example, more notice for an audit certificate
  than for a letter of guarantee).
- [config] **See the effect before you save.** A preview lists who would newly be
  warned under the new setting.
- [existing] **The Renewals look-ahead is now just a view.** Changing it filters the
  dashboard and never changes who is emailed.

### Requirements from your verified supplier list

- [config] **Requirements can come from your verified supplier list.** Download a
  template, list each supplier, its category, what you buy from it and the
  claims made, preview what each supplier would owe, and then apply.
- [config] **Nothing a person set is changed.** Rows a person set are never changed by
  an import, and nothing is deleted when a supplier drops off the list; the
  row is flagged for review instead.
- [config] **Apply a requirement packet to one or many suppliers**, from Settings or
  the supplier page, with a preview first.
- [existing] **A Needs review tab** lists requirements nobody has confirmed yet.

### Changed

- [new-orgs] **The GFSI claim now requires the audit certificate and recommends the
  audit report.** The certificate proves the scheme and its valid-through
  date; the full report is usually shared only under NDA. This applies to
  newly set-up organizations.
